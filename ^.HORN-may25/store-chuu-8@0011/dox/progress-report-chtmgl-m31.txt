# CHTMGL Progress Report (m31)
**Status:** Alpha Integration STABLE (Navigation & Windowing Polished)

## 1. Executive Summary
We have successfully implemented and polished the structural foundation for **UI Sovereignty** in GL-OS. Projects can now define nested layouts and custom coordinate systems with a clean, project-driven viewport. Navigation is fully synchronized across mouse and keyboard.

## 2. Completed Milestones

### 2.1 Structural Parsing (Alpha Foundation)
- **Nesting Support:** Elements now correctly inherit relative positions from `<panel>` and `<div>` containers.
- **Visual Sovereignty:** Implemented `ui_mode="SOVEREIGN"`. Host-side helpers are hidden to respect project identity.
- **Joystick-First UX:** Implemented scoped indexing and robust "Infinite" Number Jump logic.

### 2.2 Window Management & Polishing
- **Bounds Enforcement:** Sub-windows are strictly confined within the master window area.
- **Scoped Minimize:** The minimize button on sub-windows correctly toggles their state without affecting the global OS.
- **UI Streamlining:** Removed redundant top bar; master controls (X, F, -) centralized in the bottom toolbar.

---

## 3. Navigation Audit (RESOLVED)
The "Joystick-First" and "Number Jump" navigation logic is now 100% consistent:

1. **Mandatory Indices:** Every navigable `<button>` and `<li>` displays a unique numerical prefix.
2. **Logical Mapping:** "Number + Enter" jumps to and activates buttons based on their displayed index.
3. **Scoped Focus:** Navigation automatically resets to 1 inside "Activated" containers.
4. **Hit Detection:** Mouse and Keyboard share the same coordinate logic for selection.
