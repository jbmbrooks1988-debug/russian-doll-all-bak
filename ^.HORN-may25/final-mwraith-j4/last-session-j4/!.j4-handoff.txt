  2. Tracing the Evolution of Intentions
  The project has shifted from a "virtualized renderer" to a "sovereign
  map-composer." To understand this evolution, read the following documents in
  this specific order:

   1. _codex_dox/wraith-handoff-j2.txt (The Origin): Defines the initial bridge
      contract. Note the assumption that Wraith was a CHTMGL parser/renderer.
   2. _codex_dox/wrath-pm-codex-j2.txt (The Planning Phase): Contains the original
      super-plan. Note the lingering assumption that Wraith should be an
      "ASCII-first desktop/window manager" that replaces the CHTPML parser.
   3. _codex_dox/wraith-progress-j3.txt (The Realization): Recognizes the
      limitations of the "bridge" and the "parser" approach.
   4. wraith-recast-j4.txt (The Correction): CRITICAL. This is where the
      "Systemically False Assumption" (that Wraith is a rendering engine) is
      corrected. It redefines Wraith as a Sovereign Map-Composer adhering to the
      fuzz-op standard.
   5. post-op-feedback-j4.txt (The Refinement): Final refinement, documenting the
      shift from hardcoded windows to dynamic, transient map entities spawned on
      demand.

  Guidance for Reading: You are observing the project moving from historical
  unclarity (the attempt to reinvent the wheel via a new parser) to iterative
  clarity (the adoption of the standard TPMOS Map-Composer model). If a document
  contradicts wraith-recast-j4.txt, treat recast and post-op as the authoritative
  truth.
