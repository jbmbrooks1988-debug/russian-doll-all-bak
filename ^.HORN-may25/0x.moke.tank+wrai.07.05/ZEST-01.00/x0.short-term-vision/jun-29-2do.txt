TOP DECISION
==================================================

The current Wraith hosted-project location:

- projects/wraith/wraith-projects/

is probably not the final correct TPM/world-ownership shape.

Reason:
- per `!.world_architecture+1=rusindol.txt`, a parent/container should own its nested property inside its own root
- `wraith-alpha` is the host parent
- therefore Wraith-hosted internal projects likely belong under:

  projects/wraith-alpha/wraith-projects/

instead of as a sibling namespace under `projects/wraith/`

This should be treated as a deliberate migration step, not as an incidental path tweak.
