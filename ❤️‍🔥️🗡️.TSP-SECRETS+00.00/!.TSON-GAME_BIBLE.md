# 👻 THE SPIRITS ONLINE

### A Biblical Fantasy Tactics RPG

> "Spirits wander the Ages seeking champions. Kingdoms rise, kingdoms fall. Heroes, prophets, kings, angels, demons, and ordinary people become vessels in the eternal struggle between Light and Darkness."

---

# 🌎 CORE CONCEPT

**The Spirits Online** is a collectible tactics RPG combining:

⚔️ Tactical battles

🏰 Kingdom building

⛏️ Crafting & economy

🌍 Exploration

🤝 Trading

👥 Co-op

🏆 PvP

📖 Biblical-inspired stories

🕊️ Good vs Evil alignment

👻 Player-controlled Spirits

Playable completely offline, but enhanced online.

---

# 👻 THE PLAYER

The player is NOT a character.

The player is a **Spirit**.

Spirits possess units and guide them.

A Spirit can:

✅ Possess heroes

✅ Possess villains

✅ Lead kingdoms

✅ Influence NPCs

✅ Convert followers

✅ Discover relics

✅ Shape history

---
